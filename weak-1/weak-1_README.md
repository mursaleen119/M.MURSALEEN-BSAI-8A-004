# Week 1 solution

Dataset: [`../data/gapminder.csv`](../data/gapminder.csv). Task-specific dataset differences are noted in the notebook; no other source dataset is added.

Notebook: [`weak-1_solution.ipynb`](weak-1_solution.ipynb)

## Tasks

1. **Task 1 — Summary and shape** — Gapminder has one real dataset rather than 13 matched-statistic datasets. Country variation and continent summaries are compared honestly.
2. **Task 2 — Trends and spaghetti** — The 142-country plot is compared with continent averages and selected-country highlights; GDP per capita uses a log axis.
3. **Task 3 — Distribution and relationships** — Life expectancy and income distributions and relationships are shown by continent; no penguin fields or missing records are claimed.
4. **Task 4 — Five chart choices** — Five distinct questions use time, category, relationship, distribution and grouped comparisons, followed by a redesign.
5. **Task 5 — Time series** — Country-year records are correctly ordered; the notebook separates trend and repeated observation patterns.
