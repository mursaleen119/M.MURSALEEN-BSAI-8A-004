# Week 4 solution

Dataset: [`../data/gapminder.csv`](../data/gapminder.csv). Task-specific dataset differences are noted in the notebook; no other source dataset is added.

Notebook: [`weak-4_solution.ipynb`](weak-4_solution.ipynb)

## Tasks

1. **Task 1 — Nine time and composition cases** — Nine adaptations use Gapminder's observed country-year timeline and continent compositions.
2. **Task 2 — Windows and trend sensitivity** — A computed scan compares possible windows and reports their slope directions; units are observations, not days.
3. **Task 3 — Part-to-whole legality** — Continent GDP shares are checked as exclusive parts; the pie is compared with a sorted bar. Real-reader ranking remains pending; see reader_protocol.md.
4. **Task 4 — Four-panel review figure** — The adapted dashboard shows a time trend, continent-year heatmap, GDP composition, and country distribution.
5. **Task 5 — Written justification** — Time decisions, the missing product hierarchy, and non-applicable exclusions are documented.
6. **Task 6 — Bonus cycle plot** — The notebook shows why 12 five-year snapshots cannot support an 18-month seasonal decomposition.
