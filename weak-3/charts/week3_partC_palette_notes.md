# Part C — palette evidence

The swatch comparison calls `vizlib.simulate_cvd()` for deutan, protan and tritan; `week3_partC_palette_audit.csv` reports the worst all-pairs ΔE in each simulation and normal vision. A large palette is not automatically safer.

Course rule: use the three-slot categorical palette for no more than three categories. For more, use direct labels, marker/line styles, small multiples, or position rather than another hue. The fourth course hue has a reduced CVD/contrast margin and must be accompanied by direct labels or a table.
