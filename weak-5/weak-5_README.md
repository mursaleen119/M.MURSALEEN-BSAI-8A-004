# Week 5 solution

Dataset: [`../data/gapminder.csv`](../data/gapminder.csv). Task-specific dataset differences are noted in the notebook; no other source dataset is added.

Notebook: [`weak-5_solution.ipynb`](weak-5_solution.ipynb)

## Tasks

1. **Task 1 — Nine multivariate diagnoses** — Nine adaptations use population, GDP per capita, life expectancy and continent; geography-specific claims are not fabricated.
2. **Task 2 — Five channel and spatial decisions** — Bubble area, overplotting, scaling and PCA are computed; projections and small-area funnels are marked unavailable without geometry or counts.
3. **Task 3 — Two-map reader activity** — A reader protocol is supplied, but no map or participant results are claimed because the dataset has no boundaries; see reader_protocol.md.
4. **Task 4 — Three multivariate figures** — Bubble, parallel-coordinate and continent comparison views are built; the final panel is not mislabelled as a map.
5. **Task 5 — Written justification** — Encoding choices and the geography limitation are documented.
6. **Task 6 — Bonus normalized map** — A choropleth cannot be made from Gapminder without a second dataset, so the single-dataset rule is preserved.
